# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the contact form at https://guetix.com/#contact.
Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that
break these rules.
